# Зразок 3: батьки, які сортують гори прання
As a mom of three, I match about forty socks a week. Forty. So when my sister told me about magnetic socks, I laughed. Then I tried them. Each pair stays clipped together by a little magnet in the cuff, so sorting laundry got a lot easier. It's five pairs for $29 and they send a new set every quarter, which honestly is easier than remembering to buy socks. If you're tired of sock sorting, give SockLock a try.
