# Зразок 5: «Дорослішання» й підписка (28–40). Кут: сухий гумор, перевага підписки, а не самих шкарпеток.
At some point in your thirties you realize you're buying the same boring things on repeat. Toothpaste. Batteries. Socks. Especially socks, because half of them disappear. So here's my adult upgrade. SockLock socks have a magnet in the cuff, so each pair stays together through the wash. And instead of a sad run to the store, five pairs arrive for $29, and then a new shipment shows up every quarter. It's not exciting. It's just one less thing to think about. Honestly, that might be the most adult thing I've done all year.
