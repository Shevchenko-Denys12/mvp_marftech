# Зразок 4: Спільна пральня (25–32, орендоване житло). Кут: relatable moment з легким гумором.
Rule one of living in an apartment building: never leave your laundry unattended. Rule two: you will lose a sock anyway. I do my wash in the basement machines, and every single time I come back upstairs with a stack of clean clothes and one lonely sock. Last month I counted. Seven socks with no partners. Seven. Then a coworker showed me SockLock. There's a small magnet in the cuff, so each pair stays together in the wash. Five pairs are $29, and a new batch shows up every quarter. Laundry day is still laundry day, but at least my socks come back in pairs.
