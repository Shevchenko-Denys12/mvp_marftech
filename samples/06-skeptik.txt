# Зразок 6: Скептик і тест (25–38, пари). Кут: myth-busting, структура «сумнів → тест → висновок».
I'll be honest. When my partner ordered magnetic socks, I rolled my eyes. A magnet? In a sock? Sounded like a gimmick. So I ran a test. I snapped the cuffs together on three pairs, tossed them in the wash, and pulled them out when the cycle finished. Three pairs. Zero strays. Okay, fine. I'm convinced. SockLock is five pairs for $29, with a new shipment every quarter. Turns out the real gimmick was losing a sock every week.
